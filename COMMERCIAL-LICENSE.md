# Punk Skill Commercial Licensing

Commercial use of Punk Skill `v2.0.0` and later requires prior written
authorization and payment of a commercial license fee.

This document describes how to request a license. It is not a commercial
license and does not grant any commercial right.

## Uses that require a commercial license

A commercial license is required for organization use, employer or client
work, paid services, marketing, advertising, monetized content, brand
communication, product or workflow integration, hosted access, redistribution,
commercial datasets, model training, and any other use intended to create
revenue, compensation, operational benefit, or commercial advantage.

See [LICENSE-PERSONAL.md](./LICENSE-PERSONAL.md) for the full definition.

## Request information

Send the following information to `adrian.pduck@gmail.com`:

- legal name of the licensee;
- company or registration number, if applicable;
- billing address and contact person;
- intended use and products or services involved;
- whether the Work will be used for client work;
- number of users and deployment environments;
- requested term and territory;
- versions and components requested;
- whether modification, hosting, model training, redistribution, or
  sublicensing is required; and
- expected launch date.

## When authorization becomes effective

Commercial authorization becomes effective only when:

1. the Licensor and licensee have signed or otherwise executed a written
   commercial license identifying the authorized scope; and
2. the Licensor has received the full applicable fee, unless the executed
   license expressly provides another effective date.

A quotation, invoice, application, discussion, payment without an executed
agreement, or access to the public repository does not constitute commercial
authorization.

The commercial license should specify at least the licensee, covered Work and
versions, permitted uses, user or seat limit, client-work rights, modification
rights, Output rights, distribution and hosting rights, model-training rights,
term, territory, fee, taxes, attribution, confidentiality, warranties,
termination, audit evidence, governing law, and dispute forum.
