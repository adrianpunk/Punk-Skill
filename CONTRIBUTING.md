# Contributing to Punk Skill

Thank you for considering a contribution.

Punk Skill uses a personal-use and paid-commercial dual-licensing model. A
contribution can be accepted only after the contributor agrees to
[CLA.md](./CLA.md), confirms the provenance of all submitted material, and
provides any required third-party notices.

## Contribution requirements

1. Submit only material you created or have documented authority to submit.
2. Identify all third-party code, prompts, images, references, fonts, datasets,
   and generated assets.
3. Do not submit confidential, employer-owned, client-owned, or unlawfully
   obtained material.
4. Record the source and applicable terms of every external item in
   `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
5. State which image-generation or AI service produced generated assets and
   retain the provider terms and account record applicable on the generation
   date.
6. Sign the Contributor License Agreement before the contribution is merged.

Opening an issue or pull request does not transfer ownership and does not by
itself authorize relicensing. The maintainer must retain the signed CLA or an
equivalent verifiable electronic acceptance record.
