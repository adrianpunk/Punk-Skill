# 立体人物扁平景色纸艺

## Mode Routing

This style has exactly two output modes. Resolve one mode before compiling the image prompt:

- `before-after`: read `references/before-after.md`. Output one side-by-side source/result comparison.
- `final-artwork`: read `references/final-artwork.md`. Output one standalone final artwork.

If the user does not specify a mode, ask them to choose `前后对比图` or `单张效果图`. Do not generate both by default and never merge the two output contracts.

## Shared Visual Atom

- Use the uploaded photo as the only visual reference.
- Keep exactly one primary person as the only upright, sharp, photorealistic, fully three-dimensional subject.
- Preserve the person's recognizable identity, face, hairstyle, expression, clothes, accessories, action, gestures, body proportions, pose, original camera angle, lighting, and color. Do not redesign or beautify the person into someone else.
- Fold every non-person element backward around the contact point at the person's feet or body by about 75°–85°.
- Compress the vertical height of cars, buildings, furniture, trees, mountains, and every other environmental element to about 10%–20% of its original height. Distant scenery must be compressed even more strongly in depth.
- Make the environment parallel to the paper plane and the person perpendicular to it, with an unmistakable 90-degree spatial relationship: person up, environment lying down.
- Spread the scene broadly to the left, right, and rear as a wide, shallow oval or fan. It must connect naturally to the person's feet or body contact point.
- Retain the original scene's major recognizable elements, photographic texture, and color relationships, while slightly lowering saturation, sharpness, and contrast.
- Keep the scene interior predominantly photographic. Only its thinning outer edge may dissolve into restrained dry-brush, watercolor, or paper-fiber textures.
- Place the work on warm off-white art paper with fine fibers, subtle grain, and natural embossing. Keep the person and flattened scene centered slightly low, surrounded by generous clean negative space.
- Add only a short, soft contact shadow. Never use a long, hard, black, narrow, pointed, or dramatically angled shadow.

## Non-Negotiable Negative Constraints

- No upright non-person scenery; no normally frontal cars, buildings, furniture, trees, or mountains behind the person.
- No ordinary cutout background replacement, background blur, full-scene watercolor filter, flat collage, or person and environment sharing one vertical plane.
- No floating gap, white halo, black outline, hard cutout edge, duplicated subject, additional person, or unrelated object.
- No change to identity, face, clothes, action, gesture, body ratio, fingers, or anatomy; no cartoon, illustration, oversized head, or beauty redesign of the person.
- No text, frame, border, logo, watermark, pure black silhouette, or decorative typography.
