# 单张效果图模式

Use this mode only when the user asks for `效果图`, `单张效果图`, `成品图`, `final artwork`, or `single image`.

## Output Contract

Create exactly one standalone surreal paper-art image from the uploaded photo. Default aspect ratio: `3:4`. If the user specifies another ratio, keep it. Output only the final transformed artwork—never include the source photo, a comparison, split screen, diptych, grid, or multiple images.

## Person

Precisely isolate the person from the uploaded photo. The person is the only clear, photorealistic, fully three-dimensional subject. Preserve recognizable identity, face, hairstyle, expression, clothes, accessories, action, gestures, body proportions, pose, camera angle, lighting, and original colors as tightly as possible. Do not beautify the person into someone else or redesign the face, clothes, pose, or anatomy.

## Flattened Scene

Compress everything except the person—including cars, buildings, roads, mountains, sea, trees, tables, chairs, and all other objects—into one shallow two-dimensional scene attached to the paper.

Use the person's feet or body contact point as the hinge. Fold the entire environment backward around that horizontal axis by roughly 75°–85°. Formerly upright cars, buildings, and other scenery must lie down with obvious depth compression. Show only compressed tops, outlines, and shortened façades; reduce environmental vertical height to roughly 10%–20% and compress distant content even more strongly.

Keep the person perpendicular to the paper and the environment parallel to it, forming an unmistakable 90-degree contrast: the person rises; the environment lies flat. Spread the scenery broadly left, right, and rear from the contact point as a wide, shallow oval or fan. Never turn it into a thin, sharp, exaggerated diagonal projection.

Retain the source photo's major scene elements, colors, and spatial relationships. Slightly reduce environmental saturation, sharpness, and contrast. Keep its interior predominantly photographic; let only the thinning perimeter dissolve into subtle dry-brush, watercolor, or paper-fiber texture. Do not watercolor the entire environment.

Join the person's feet or body naturally to the flattened world. Add a short, soft contact shadow so the person appears to rise from the paper. Do not leave a floating gap, white edge, black edge, or hard cutout trace.

## Paper and Composition

Place the work on warm off-white art paper with fine fibers, subtle grain, and natural embossing. Position the person and flattened scene centered slightly low, occupying about 30%–40% of the canvas. Preserve broad, clean negative space above and around the work.

The final piece should feel quiet, restrained, spatial, and premium: surreal photography, paper-art installation, pop-up book, and high-end editorial illustration.

## Strictly Avoid

- Any upright non-person scenery; normal frontal cars, buildings, furniture, trees, or mountains; or an ordinary background behind the person.
- Standard cutout replacement, blurred background, simple watercolor-edge treatment, full-scene watercolor, flat collage, or person and scenery on one vertical plane.
- Long hard shadows, black silhouettes, narrow pointed projections, floating discontinuities, cutout halos, borders, frames, text, logos, or watermarks.
- Any change to identity, face, hairstyle, expression, clothes, accessories, action, gestures, fingers, anatomy, body proportions, camera angle, lighting, or color.
- Cartoonization, illustration, oversized head, duplicated subject, extra person, or unrelated content.

Generate exactly one final artwork and nothing else.
