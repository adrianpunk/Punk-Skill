# 前后对比图模式

Use this mode only when the user asks for `前后对比图`, `对比图`, `before/after`, or `comparison`.

## Output Contract

Create one clean side-by-side comparison image with two equal visual panels and a narrow neutral divider:

- Left panel: reproduce the uploaded source photo unchanged in composition and content. Do not retouch, recolor, crop away the person, add labels, or add decorative framing.
- Right panel: show the transformed surreal paper-art result described below.
- Keep the same person at a comparable scale and placement across both panels so the transformation reads immediately.
- Default aspect ratio: `3:2` for the combined comparison canvas. If the user specifies another ratio, keep it.
- Do not add `before`, `after`, arrows, captions, titles, logos, or watermarks.

## Right-Panel Transformation

Precisely isolate the person from the uploaded photo. The person is the only upright, clear, photorealistic, fully three-dimensional subject. Preserve recognizable identity, face, hairstyle, expression, clothing, accessories, pose, gestures, body proportions, camera angle, lighting, and original colors as tightly as possible. Do not redesign the face, clothes, pose, or anatomy.

Flatten everything except the person—including cars, buildings, restaurants, roads, mountains, sea, tables, chairs, trees, and other objects—into one shallow two-dimensional scene attached to the paper. Around the horizontal axis at the person's feet or body contact point, fold the whole environment backward by roughly 75°–85°. Formerly upright scenery must show strong perspective shortening, with only compressed tops, outlines, and shortened façades visible. Compress all environmental vertical height to roughly 10%–20%; increase depth compression with distance.

The person must be perpendicular to the paper while the environment lies parallel to it, creating a clear 90-degree relationship. Spread the flattened scene broadly from the contact point toward both sides and the rear as a wide, shallow oval or fan. Join the scene naturally to the body without a floating break, cutout halo, black edge, or white outline.

Preserve the source scene's major recognizable elements, photographic texture, and original color relationships. Slightly reduce environmental saturation, sharpness, and contrast. Keep the interior photographic; let only the thinning perimeter dissolve into restrained dry-brush, watercolor, or paper-fiber edges. Do not turn the whole environment into watercolor.

Place the transformed work on warm off-white art paper with fine fibers, subtle grain, and natural embossing. Keep the subject group centered slightly low, occupying about 30%–40% of the right panel, with generous clean negative space above and around it. Add a short, soft contact shadow that makes the person rise convincingly from the paper.

The right panel should feel like surreal photography, a paper-art installation, a pop-up book, and a refined editorial illustration.

## Strictly Avoid

- Any upright non-person element or normal frontal background behind the person.
- A standard background replacement, blurred background, flat photo collage, or simple watercolor-edge filter.
- A long, hard, black, narrow, pointed, or dramatically angled shadow.
- Changes to identity, face, hair, expression, clothing, accessories, pose, gesture, fingers, anatomy, body proportions, camera angle, lighting, or color.
- Cartoonization, illustration, oversized head, copied person, extra person, text, labels, border, logo, or watermark.

Generate exactly one combined before/after comparison image and no additional alternatives.
